Delivery by area

Rentals and delivery are available on Grand Cayman only. Delivery is available for cranes, boom trucks, telehandlers, tools and rental equipment. For other equipment, the area fee applies separately to each rental item delivered. Post shores, ground protection mats, ICF braces, scaffolding and Ellis wall braces share one delivery fee per order at the selected area rate, regardless of quantity or duration. All CCC-collected rental items also incur one pickup/collection fee per order, except cranes, boom trucks, telehandlers and attachments/add-ons travelling with them. Weekly and monthly delivery waivers apply to other rentals; collection is charged separately. Customers may also collect tools, generators and accessories. Complete rental sets are charged as one item.

Operator overtime

More than 45 hours worked per week is considered overtime. Work outside the standard rental schedule is charged at the current term rate plus CI$25.00 per operator/hour. Public Holidays are charged at the current term rate plus CI$50.00 per operator/hour.

    Cayman Crane Company Rental & Service Terms

    1. Request and acceptance. This checkout submits a booking request only. Equipment, operators and dates are not reserved until Cayman Crane Company Ltd. (“CCC”) issues written acceptance.
    2. Rates. By accepting these terms, I understand that the displayed total is an estimate and that final charges are confirmed by Cayman Crane Company. Rates shown are estimates based on the selected rental term and quantity. Loyalty discounts are rounded to the nearest whole CI dollar. The four-hour minimum applies to hourly crane and boom-truck bookings. Delivery area fees apply per item to eligible equipment and tool rentals when delivery is selected; post shores, ground protection mats, ICF braces, scaffolding and Ellis wall braces share one delivery fee per order regardless of quantity or duration. All CCC-collected rental items also incur one collection fee per order, except cranes, boom trucks, telehandlers and attachments/add-ons travelling with them. Each trip uses the selected area rate when CCC provides transport. Weekly and monthly delivery fees are waived for other rentals; collection is charged separately. Customers can collect tools and other rental items. Included components of a complete rental set are not charged separately for delivery. Final charges may also include overtime, fuel, rigging, standby time, permits, damage, cleaning and other agreed costs.
    3. Payment. Any online payment is a deposit or authorization toward the final approved booking and does not itself confirm availability. The customer authorizes CCC to invoice approved additional charges. Deposits are applied to the final invoice.
    4. Site conditions. Rentals and services are available on Grand Cayman only. The customer must provide safe and lawful access, adequate working space, suitable ground bearing capacity, accurate load information, required permits and unobstructed setup areas. CCC may stop or refuse work it considers unsafe.
    5. Operations and safety. CCC retains operational control of its equipment and personnel. The customer and all site personnel must follow the operator’s and lift supervisor’s safety directions and remain clear of restricted areas.
    6. Customer care of rental equipment. For bare rentals and accessories, the customer is responsible for secure storage, normal daily care and returning the equipment in the condition received, ordinary wear excepted. Loss, theft, misuse and avoidable damage may be charged to the customer.
    7. Full-tank fuel return. Return the gasoline- or diesel-powered rental items listed in the fuel return section with a full tank of their specified fuel. This requirement does not apply to cranes or boom trucks.
    8. Changes and cancellation. Schedule changes depend on availability. Mobilization, special-order, permit, labor and cancellation costs already incurred may be charged. Weather and unsafe conditions may require rescheduling.
    9. Liability. To the extent permitted by Cayman Islands law, the customer is responsible for loss or damage caused by inaccurate information, unsafe site conditions, unauthorized use or failure to follow instructions. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss except where law does not allow exclusion.
    10. Insurance and authorization. The customer confirms that it is authorized to place this request and will maintain insurance reasonably appropriate to the work. CCC may request identification, a signed credit application, proof of insurance or additional security before acceptance.
    11. Entire booking. CCC’s written quotation or booking confirmation, these terms and any project-specific lift plan form the agreement. Project-specific written terms prevail if inconsistent.

    *Additional hours worked outside of the 4 hour minimum will be billed at the current hourly rate*

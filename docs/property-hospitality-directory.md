# Cayman property and hospitality company directory

Researched 13 November 2026. Includes 41 company choices across hotels, condos, property stratas, property managers, strata managers and property consultants in the Cayman Islands. Each has a publicly published business-domain email address and evidence of Cayman operations. This is a researched directory, not an exhaustive list or certification of legal registration.

The [CSV](../public/cayman-property-hospitality-companies.csv) contains names, @domains, published addresses, categories, locations and source links. The [research data](../data/cayman-property-hospitality-companies.json) retains the same evidence and exclusions. Cloudflare-obfuscated email addresses for THPM, Beachcomber and Avalon were read from the original contact-page HTML.

The published email domain is used, which can differ from the website: Davenport Property Management & Services uses @dpms.ky and Kuavo uses @kuavo.com. Grand Caymanian and Holiday Inn share a single entry because their published contact domain is the same. Hotel Indigo's shared Seafire domain is represented by the Seafire entry. No global hotel-chain domain or consumer mailbox provider was assigned to a particular Cayman property. Individual condo units marketed through rental agents were not given ownership of their agents' domains.

The import skips existing name/domain conflicts and preserves current company names, account restrictions, loyalty balances and history. It creates no customer logins and sends no messages. Customers must still verify their own work email before accessing company rewards. Existing admin controls can edit company names and domains.

Validation includes repeat imports, existing reward-balance preservation, signup recommendations for all 41 domains, rejection of spoofed domain suffixes, and checks that no users or account emails are created by the import.

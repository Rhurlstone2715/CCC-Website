# Pending booking rewards

The customer account displays earned points separately from pending points on the existing rewards card. Each pending/approved booking shows estimated pending points and whether proof was received. Proof upload alone does not earn points; an administrator must confirm payment. The admin booking detail also shows this distinction.

Pending points use the current estimate total in KYD, rounded down to whole points (one point per KYD $1). Unpriced estimates show "to be confirmed". Cancelled/declined bookings are excluded. The pending total covers all relevant bookings, not just the latest 50 displayed on the account. Verified company members share the pending total under the same membership rules as earned rewards; separately rewarded government sub-companies remain separate. No colleague booking details are exposed.

Existing earned rewards and tier eligibility continue to use only admin-confirmed PAID amounts plus explicit admin reward credit. Pending totals are display-only and never feed checkout discounts, tier progress or estimates. Confirming payment replaces the pending estimate with earned points based on the actual confirmed amount; reopening removes those earned points and restores pending status. Revised estimates automatically update pending points.
